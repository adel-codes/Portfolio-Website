How we practise

Five commitments, kept.

  1. We assess before we prescribe. If the assessment does not support treatment, we say so.
  2. We tell you the regulatory status and the state of the evidence for any medicine we recommend.
  3. We document consent in plain language, with costs, before anything begins.
  4. We review on a schedule, and we stop when stopping is the right decision.
  5. We refer when someone else is better placed to help you.
