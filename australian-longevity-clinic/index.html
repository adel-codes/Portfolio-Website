I

What this is

The longevity field is crowded with promises. This is a medical practice.

Australian Longevity Clinic is led by AHPRA-registered doctors and operates within Australian therapeutic goods regulations. Every patient has a medical consultation before any treatment, pathology where it is needed, a written plan, and scheduled review. If a treatment is not indicated for you, we say so.

You will not find a menu of prescription medicines on this website. Prescription-only medicines cannot lawfully be advertised to the public in Australia, and a medicine should be chosen for a person after assessment rather than before it. What is suitable for you is discussed in consultation, with the evidence for it and the limits of that evidence.

  • Doctor-ledEvery treatment is prescribed and overseen by a registered medical practitioner.
  • AHPRA-registeredAll treating doctors hold current registration with the Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency.
  • Consultation firstNo treatment proceeds without a medical consultation and, where needed, pathology.
  • In clinic, in BrisbaneCare is delivered in person at 628 Ipswich Road, Annerley, with review built into every program.

IV

Standards you can check

Trust is built on things you can verify.

The longevity field is crowded with claims. These are the standards we hold ourselves to, and how to check them for yourself.

Registration
Every treating doctor holds current registration with AHPRA. Confirm any practitioner on the public register at ahpra.gov.au.
Regulation
Medicines are prescribed and supplied in accordance with Therapeutic Goods Administration requirements, including those for compounded medicines prepared for an individual patient.
Consent
You receive the known benefits, risks, alternatives and costs in writing before treatment, and your consent is documented.
Evidence
We distinguish between what is well established, what is emerging and what is unknown, and we tell you which is which.
Privacy
Your health information is handled under the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles.
Advertising
No patient testimonials and no before-and-after images, in keeping with the National Law for regulated health services. Read our patient policies.
