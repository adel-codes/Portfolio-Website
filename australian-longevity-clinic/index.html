02 How care works here

A consultation before anything else.

The order matters. We assess first, prescribe only where it is indicated, and review whatever we prescribe.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    An unhurried appointment with one of our doctors to understand your history, your goals, and what is realistic.

  2. 02

    Assessment

    Pathology and clinical measures where they are needed, so that decisions rest on your results rather than on a menu.

  3. 03

    Program

    A written plan covering what is prescribed, why, how it is administered, what it costs and what to expect.

  4. 04

    Review

    Scheduled follow-up to measure what has changed, adjust the plan and, where appropriate, stop.

If a treatment is not indicated for you, we will say so. That is part of the service, not a failure of it.A clinic commitment

03 Our standards

Trust is built on things you can check.

The longevity field is crowded with claims. These are the standards we hold ourselves to, and how you can verify them.

Registration
Every treating doctor holds current registration with AHPRA. You can confirm any practitioner on the public register at ahpra.gov.au.
Regulation
Medicines are prescribed and supplied in accordance with Therapeutic Goods Administration requirements, including those for compounded medicines prepared for an individual patient.
Consent
You receive the known benefits, risks, alternatives and costs in writing before treatment, and your consent is documented.
Evidence
We distinguish between what is well established, what is emerging and what is unknown, and we tell you which is which.
Privacy
Your health information is handled under the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles.
Advertising
We do not publish patient testimonials or before-and-after images, in keeping with the advertising requirements of the National Law for regulated health services.

05 Questions

Asked often.

Anything not covered here, ask reception. Clinical questions are best answered in consultation.

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly. A consultation with one of our doctors is required before any treatment, and if you have recent pathology or a letter from your GP, bring it with you.

Is treatment suitable for everyone?

No. Suitability is determined in consultation, and some people will be advised against a treatment they came in for. We would rather lose a booking than treat someone who should not be treated.

Are the medicines TGA-approved?

Some are approved products. Some therapies use compounded medicines prepared for an individual patient on a doctor's prescription, which have not been evaluated by the TGA in the same way. Your doctor will explain the regulatory status of anything prescribed and what that means for you.

What does it cost?

Consultation fees and program costs are provided in writing before you commit to anything. Where a Medicare or private health rebate may apply, reception will tell you in advance.

How long is an appointment?

Initial consultations are unhurried; allow the time reception advises when you book. Infusions typically take between 30 and 90 minutes in addition to any consultation.

Where do I park?

Details on parking and access are sent with your booking confirmation. The clinic is on Ipswich Road and is easy to reach from Brisbane's inner south and the CBD.
