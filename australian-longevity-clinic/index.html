IV therapy

A health service given in the clinic, under the direct supervision of its doctors.

What it is
Infusions of fluids, vitamins, iron or NAD+, given in the clinic.
First step
A consultation. Iron also needs pathology from the last 3 months and a GP referral.
Time
30 minutes to 4 hours, by infusion.
Where
In the chair at Annerley, under a doctor's supervision.

IV therapy in detail Begin with this program

Peptide programs

Peptides are short chains of amino acids that act as signals in the body.

What it is
Prescription peptide programs built around one goal, such as recovery, sleep or body composition.
First step
A consultation. Most therapeutic peptides are Schedule 4 medicines, prescribed only if suitable.
Time
Weeks to months, reviewed as you go.
Where
Prescribed by the clinic's doctors, with administration taught.

Peptide programs in detail Begin with this program

Testosterone therapy

For men with symptoms whose low testosterone is confirmed by pathology.

What it is
Testosterone replacement for men with confirmed deficiency.
First step
A consultation, then pathology to confirm. Symptoms alone are not enough.
Time
Ongoing, with repeat pathology and regular review.
Where
Prescribed and monitored by the clinic's doctors.

Testosterone therapy in detail Begin with this program

Medical weight management

For adults for whom weight has become a medical question.

What it is
Medical weight management: an assessment, a plan, and prescription medicines only where appropriate.
First step
A consultation and pathology.
Time
Months, read as a trend rather than a weigh-in.
Where
Prescribed and monitored by the clinic's doctors.
