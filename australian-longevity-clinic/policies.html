The clinic · Patient policies

How we work with you.

The policies below set out what you can expect from us and what we ask of you. They are written to be read, and reception can answer questions about any of them.

Privacy

We collect health information so that we can assess, treat and follow you up safely. It is handled in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. Your records are stored securely, accessed only by staff involved in your care or in running the clinic, and are not disclosed to anyone else without your consent unless the law requires it.

You may ask to see your records, request a correction, or ask how your information has been used. Requests go to reception in writing, and we respond within a reasonable time.

Appointments and cancellations

Consultations are by appointment. If you need to change or cancel, please give at least 24 hours' notice so that the time can be offered to someone else. Late cancellations and missed appointments may attract a fee, which reception will explain when you book.

If you are running late, call or email as early as you can. We will do our best to accommodate you, but a shortened appointment may need to be rebooked so that your care is not rushed.

Fees and payment

Fees for consultations and programs are provided in writing before you commit to anything. Consultation fees are payable on the day. Where Medicare or private health fund rebates may apply, we tell you in advance; many of our services are not rebated, and compounded medicines are not subsidised under the Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme.

Medicines dispensed by a pharmacy are charged by the pharmacy. Pathology is billed by the laboratory.

Informed consent

Before any treatment you receive, in writing, the proposed treatment, its purpose, the known benefits and risks, the alternatives including no treatment, the regulatory status of any medicine, and the costs. You have time to ask questions, and your consent is documented. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Where a treatment uses a compounded medicine, we tell you that it has not been evaluated by the Therapeutic Goods Administration in the way approved products have.

Feedback and complaints

If something has not gone well, please tell us. Speak to reception or write to reception@australianlongevityclinic.com.au marked for the attention of the Practice Manager. We acknowledge complaints promptly, investigate them, and tell you the outcome.

You may also raise a concern with the Office of the Health Ombudsman, Queensland's independent health complaints agency, on 133 646 or at oho.qld.gov.au, or about an individual practitioner with the Australian Health Practitioner Regulation Agency at ahpra.gov.au.

About this website

Information on this website is general in nature and is not medical advice. It does not describe specific prescription medicines, in keeping with the Therapeutic Goods Advertising Code, and it does not include patient testimonials, in keeping with the National Law governing registered health practitioners. Whether any treatment is appropriate for you can only be decided in consultation with a doctor.

Questions about any of this?

Reception can walk you through fees, appointments and what to expect. Clinical questions are best answered in consultation.
