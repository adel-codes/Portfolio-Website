Before you begin

What happens during the consultation?

It's a phone call with one of the clinic's doctors, usually 15 to 25 minutes. The doctor will already have read your questionnaire, so the call goes on your symptoms, your goals and your questions before anything is recommended.

Why is there a $75 fee?

It pays for the doctor's time reading your questionnaire before the call, the medical assessment on the call, and ongoing support from your treating doctor.

When will the doctor call?

Within 1 to 2 business days of your form and payment arriving, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm AEST, on the number you give.

Do I need to prepare anything?

No. If you have recent blood tests or medical reports, keep them nearby for the call, but you don't need them.

Is the consultation bulk billed?

No. The $75 is a private consultation fee, paid when you send the questionnaire. Some consultations may attract a Medicare rebate, depending on their clinical nature.

What can happen after the consultation?

Depending on your assessment, the doctor may recommend IV therapy, a peptide program, testosterone therapy, medical weight management or a combination. They may also recommend pathology first, or nothing at all.

Is my health information confidential?

Yes. It is handled under Australian privacy law and the clinic's privacy policy, and used for your care.

Do I need a referral?

Not for most programs. Iron infusions need a GP referral and pathology from the last 3 months; the clinic can arrange a pathology referral if you don't have recent results.
