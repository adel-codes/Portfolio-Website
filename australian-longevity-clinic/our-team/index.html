How they practise

The clinic's doctors bring experience across general medicine, integrative wellness, women's health, men's health and preventive care. They take the time to understand your history, goals and any contraindications before recommending anything.

Wellness and medicine are not separate disciplines here. Every recommendation is grounded in a clinical assessment and individual need, and the doctors say clearly what a service can and can't do.

The team keeps up with the evidence in integrative medicine, IV therapy and peptide science through ongoing professional development.
