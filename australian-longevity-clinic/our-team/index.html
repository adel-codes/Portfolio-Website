How they practise

The doctor reads your questionnaire before the call. On the call they go through your history and your goals, and check for anything that would make a treatment unsafe for you.

Whatever they recommend comes out of that assessment. If a program can't do what you want it to, the doctor will say so, and will tell you what the evidence does and doesn't show.

The doctors keep up with the evidence on IV therapy, peptides and integrative medicine through ongoing professional development.
