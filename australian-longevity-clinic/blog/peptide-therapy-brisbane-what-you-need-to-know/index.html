Peptide Therapy in Brisbane: How It Works and the Peptides We Use

Peptide therapy uses synthetic peptides that closely mimic signalling molecules the body makes itself. This is how it works at our Brisbane clinic, and how much is known about the peptides our doctors prescribe.

The human body naturally produces hundreds of different peptides, each with its own function. They are short chains of amino acids (the building blocks of proteins) that act as biological signalling molecules, interacting directly with receptors to trigger specific responses such as tissue repair, hormone release, immune modulation and cellular regeneration.

Production of many key peptides declines with age, and this may contribute to slower recovery, reduced muscle mass, increased body fat and impaired sleep. Peptide therapy aims to restore these signalling pathways using pharmaceutical-grade synthetic peptides that closely mimic the body's own molecules.

Every peptide program at our Brisbane clinic (628 Ipswich Road, Annerley) is prescribed and supervised by our registered medical practitioners, and treatment plans are individualised based on a thorough health assessment, blood work and your specific wellness goals.

How peptides are given

That depends on the compound and the treatment goal. The most common method is subcutaneous injection: a small injection under the skin that you give yourself, similar to an insulin injection. Certain compounds are given by intramuscular injection, into muscle tissue. Some peptides, such as Selank, are nasal sprays absorbed through the nasal mucosa, and some, such as GHK-Cu, come as a topical cream applied directly to the skin.

Our medical team will guide you through the technique for your prescribed protocol and provide full training and support materials.

Who it may suit, and who it doesn't

Peptide therapy may be appropriate for adults seeking support with:

  • chronic injury recovery and musculoskeletal repair
  • healthy ageing and cellular longevity
  • sleep quality and growth hormone support
  • immune function and gut health
  • cognitive wellness
  • skin quality and hair health

A thorough medical assessment is required before any peptide program is prescribed. It starts with our online health questionnaire: a doctor reads your answers and contacts you within 1–2 business days. Nothing is prescribed from the questionnaire alone. Your doctor reviews your full health history and any relevant blood work before recommending anything, and sometimes the answer is no.

Peptide therapy is not appropriate for anyone who is pregnant or breastfeeding, or for people with certain hormone-sensitive conditions.
